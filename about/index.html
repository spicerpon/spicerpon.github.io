About KOGAMES

KOGAMES is an independent game studio from Korea. We make small games with long tails: idle and casual games for Android that you can play with one hand, and, since autumn 2026, browser games that run on any device with nothing to install. This page is the long version of who we are, what we have made and how we work.

How KOGAMES started

KOGAMES began in 2025 with one simple idea: the games we most wanted to make were the ones we kept returning to ourselves — easy to pick up for a minute, rewarding to come back to for months. That idea became three idle games on Google Play: Star Miner, an idle space-mining game, the pixel-art idle RPG GunGon, and Wormball, where you grow an asteroid. They are played by people in dozens of countries, which is why our games ship in several languages from day one.

In October 2026 we started building for the browser as well. Browser games remove the biggest barrier a small studio faces — the install — and let us try ideas quickly. Deep Bite, a multiplayer fish .io game, came first, then the merge puzzle Candy Merge 2048 and the block puzzle Block Pop, all playable at kogames.dev in the time it takes to load a page.

How we work

  • Easy to start, hard to put down. Every game is designed around a loop you understand in ten seconds and keep discovering for weeks. If a feature needs a tutorial longer than one screen, we cut the feature.
  • We read everything. Store reviews, support emails and Discord threads are read by the people who make the games, and they decide what goes into the next update. The update notes for each game show that trail.
  • No tricks. Our mobile games are free with optional ads and purchases; our browser games are free with optional rewarded ads. Nothing is hidden behind a timer you have to pay to skip, and watching an ad is always a choice.
  • Light on data. We collect only what a game needs to run — a best score for a ranking, an anonymous session for multiplayer. The details are written down in plain language in our privacy policy.
  • Built to last. The browser games are plain HTML5 and JavaScript with no framework and no install, so they will keep working on next year's devices too.

Community and contact

The quickest way to reach us is email: spicerpon@gmail.com. We answer in English, Korean and Japanese, usually within a few days. Press, portals and partners: use the same email. We are happy to share screenshots, icons and descriptions, and our browser games can be embedded or distributed with attribution — ask and we will send what you need.
