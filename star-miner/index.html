Signing in with Google

How Star Miner uses your Google Account

Why we ask you to sign in

Star Miner uses Google Sign-In only to identify your player account and to save and restore your game progress — for example, when you move to a new phone.

What we access

Only the basic profile Google shares at sign-in: your account ID, name, email address and profile picture. We never access your Gmail, Drive files, contacts or any other Google account data.

What we never do

We do not sell your Google account data or use it for advertising. Read the full Privacy Policy.
